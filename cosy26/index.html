Canadian Operator Symposium

June 1 to June 5, 2026

Carleton University, Ottawa


The 53rd anniversary of the Canadian Operator Symposium (COSy) will be held at Carleton University, on the unceded territory of the Algonquin Anishinaabe nation. We will take the opportunity at this COSy to celebrate the eightieth birthday of George Elliott.

If you would like to give a contributed talk, please indicate this on the registration form. There is a deadline of May 8 to apply to give a contributed talk.
