Travel and accommodation

Travelling to Ottawa

Getting from the airport to Carleton University by public transportation (light rail train) takes about 30 minutes. Take the train until it kicks you off at South Keys, then take another train south towards Bayview, getting off at Carleton U. A taxi from the airport to Carleton University costs around $35.
You can also reach Ottawa by Via rail. To get from the train station to Carleton University by light rail takes about 45 minutes. Take the train west towards Tunney's Pasture, transfer to Line 2 at Bayview, then take the train south to Carleton U. (A quicker bus option may be found with the OC transpo route planner.

Accommodation

Carleton University is located near downtown Ottawa. Most of the nearest hotel accommodation is downtown Ottawa; if staying there, we recommend using the light rail train system to get to Carleton. Accommodation in residence on campus is also available (see below).
A few (of many) accommodation options:

  • Holiday Inn Parliament Hill. Rate is $250/night including taxes for a 1-king bed standard room. We have blocked off rooms and have this rate available until May 1. To book use this link, or alternatively call the hotel at 613-236-1133; ask for “In House Reservations”, then quote the group code COS or reference Canadian Operator Symposium. Downtown, about 4km from Carleton.
  • LIV extended stay. Rate is $319/night plus taxes for a 1-bedroom suite (subject to change depending on availability. To book you may email concierge@irent.com. Downtown, about 2.5km from Carleton.
