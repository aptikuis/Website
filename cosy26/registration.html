Canadian Operator Symposium

June 1 to June 5, 2026

Carleton University, Ottawa


Registration


Registration for COSy 2022 is through Eventbrite; please click here. There is a registration fee of $200 (profs with research grants), $100 (profs without research grants), $50 (postdocs, grad students, etc.). After paying the registration fee, you may use the registration form to indicate if you would like to give a contributed talk, and to indicate whether you would like to apply for travel support.

Travel support

We may be able to offer some travel funding support to postdocs and graduate students. Likely this support will be in the form of providing accommodation in on-campus residence. There is a deadline of May 8 for applying for travel funding support and for contributed talks.

With support from the Fields Institute.
